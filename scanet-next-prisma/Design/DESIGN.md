# Design System Strategy

## Brand Identity & Color Philosophy
Our visual identity is anchored in trust and professional clarity. The primary palette utilizes a deep, authoritative blue (#0057B8) that conveys stability and reliability. This is supported by a sophisticated slate secondary tone (#536370) for functional UI elements and a crisp, neutral foundation (#F0F4F8) that ensures a clean, modern aesthetic. The light color mode provides a bright, accessible environment for productivity and information density.

## Typography
The system employs a dual-font approach to balance character with utility:
- **Headlines:** Manrope is used for headlines, offering a modern, geometric feel that remains approachable and clean.
- **Body & Labels:** Inter is the workhorse for all body text and interface labels, chosen for its exceptional legibility and neutral personality at various scales.

## Shape and Spatial Rhythm
The interface moves away from hyper-rounded or pill-shaped aesthetics toward a more structured and balanced look.
- **Roundedness:** Set to **Moderate (2)**, UI components feature softened corners that feel friendly yet professional, avoiding the playfulness of maximum rounding while maintaining a contemporary edge.
- **Spacing:** The system utilizes **Normal (2)** spacing, providing a standard, comfortable breathing room between elements that supports standard data-entry and content consumption workflows without feeling overly airy or cramped.

## Component Execution
Buttons, cards, and input fields follow the moderate corner radius, creating a consistent visual language across the platform. The primary blue is reserved for key calls to action, while the neutral and secondary tones manage the background hierarchy and supporting information.
